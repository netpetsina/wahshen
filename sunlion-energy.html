Why solar in Singapore

A rooftop that earns while you work.

Singapore sits near the equator with strong year-round irradiation — and rooftop solar is already the country's biggest renewable resource. Here is what a well-designed system can do for you.

  • Electricity that pays for itself

    At today's panel costs, a well-sized home system can pay back in as little as 5 years, then keep producing for 25+.

  • Cut grid dependence, sell the surplus

    Offset your daytime consumption and export excess generation back to the grid through SP Group.

  • Rooftop is Singapore's solar backbone

    More than 80% of national capacity sits on rooftops — from landed homes to HDB blocks, JTC industrial estates and malls.

  • Green credentials that count

    For businesses: measurable carbon abatement, RECs, and stronger BCA Green Mark and ESG reporting.

Good questions

Solar in Singapore, demystified.

Short answers grounded in official sources — EMA, MTI and BCA.

For landed homes and most commercial roofs, a permit application through a Qualified Person (covering URA/BCA requirements) is required before installation. SUNLION ENERGY GROUP prepares and submits all paperwork as part of the project — you don't have to deal with the authorities yourself.
With declining panel costs, the payback period for a home solar system can now be as short as about 5 years (EMA, 2025). Typical systems land between 5–9 years depending on roof size, orientation, your consumption and the electricity tariff at the time.
EMA estimates rooftop PV at roughly S$1,000–1,600 per kWp — smaller residential systems sit near the higher end, while large industrial rooftops can approach S$1,000 per kWp. We give a fixed, itemised quote after the site survey.
Yes. Grid-connected systems can export surplus generation through SP Group under the prevailing scheme, and owners can also register and sell renewable energy certificates (RECs) generated by their system.
Very little, but not nothing. Typical operation and maintenance for residential systems runs around 1–2% of system cost per year — periodic panel cleaning, inverter checks and monitoring. SUNLION ENERGY GROUP's maintenance plan covers all of it, with alerts if output drops.
Industry-standard coverage: modules typically carry a 25-year performance warranty (output guaranteed at ~80–85% after 25 years), inverters 5–10 years, and SUNLION ENERGY GROUP adds a 10-year workmanship warranty covering roof penetrations and installation quality.
